About me

I am a third-year PhD student at the IMDEA Software Institute, supervised by Thaleia Dimitra Doudali, working on ML for Systems.

Previously, I received my MSc in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the University of Thessaly, where I did my diploma thesis on Resource Management on the Computing Continuum.

My research interests include cloud computing and systems for ML.
